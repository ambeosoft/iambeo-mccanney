Urgent eye problem?

For flashes and floaters, severe eye pain or an eye injury, call us immediately on 028 8225 9636. If we are closed, contact your GP out-of-hours service or go to the nearest emergency department. Call 999 for sudden loss of vision or a serious eye injury.
